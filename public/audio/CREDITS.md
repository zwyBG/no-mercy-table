Announcer voices

All complete phrases are synthesized with the same macOS Daniel voice at 210 words per minute. Each phrase is generated in a single pass; no mixing of Add with a different number speaker. All phrases use the same playback rate, compression, and echo. Browser-generated bass and impact tones remain separate effects.

voice-lines.json lists every spoken phrase. The earlier Kenney numeric recordings have been replaced to keep pronunciation and timbre consistent.
